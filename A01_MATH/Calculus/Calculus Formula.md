3. $\dfrac{d}{dx}(ux) =u\dfrac{dv}{dx} + v\dfrac{du}{dx}$

	 $\dfrac{d}{dx}f(x)g(x)= f(x)·g'(x) + g(x)·f'(x)$

4. $\dfrac{d}{dx}(\dfrac{u}{v}) =\dfrac{v\dfrac{du}{dx} - u\dfrac{dv}{dx}}{v^2}$

---

4. $\dfrac{d}{dx}x^n = nx^{n-1}$
	
	$\dfrac{d}{dx}x^{7} = (7x)'x^{7-1} = 7x^{6}$
