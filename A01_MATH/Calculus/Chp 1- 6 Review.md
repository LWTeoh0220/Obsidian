1. \quad &\
 \text{Let } f\left(\frac{x+1}{1-x}\right) = 2x + 3. \text{ Find } f(x). \\

&\text{let }\frac{x+1}{1-x} = y \\
&\Rightarrow x+1= y-xy \\
&\Rightarrow x =\frac{y-1}{y+1}\\
&\because f(\frac{x+1}{1-x}) = 2x +3 \\
&\Rightarrow f(y) = 2(\frac{y-1}{y+1}) + 3 \\
 \end{aligned}$$
