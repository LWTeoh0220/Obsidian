# Topic：期望值、随机变数之函数、条件机率分布与失意性

## 6-1 期望值 Expectation
- 做随机试验时，我们很希望能有某种估算
- 平均值是我们平常最常普遍的估算值
- 作1-n次实验的平均值$\frac{X_1+X_2+X_3+\dots+X_n}{n}$都是随机变数
- 当试验次数无限多时，实验的平均值将收敛到**常数**

- 多次实验的样本空间为{$1,2,\dots,n$}。作实验N次，记录各结果出现次数，分别为$N_1,N_2,\dots,N_n$
- 平均值(Mean)：$\sum\limits_{x=1}^n\dfrac{xN_x}{N}$
	- 带入大数法则：
	 $\lim\limits_{N \to \infty}\dfrac{N_X}{N} = P_X(x) \Rightarrow \lim\limits_{N \to \infty}mean =\lim\limits_{N \to \infty}\sum\limits_{x=1}^nx\dfrac{N_x}{N}=\sum\limits_{x=1}^nx\times P_X(x)$

 Mean值又称作期望值

#### 随机变数的函数期望值
g(X)期望值定义为
$$E[g(X)] = \sum\limits_{x=-\infty}^\infty g(x)\times P_X(x)$$
$$E[\alpha g(X)] = \alpha\sum\limits_{x=-\infty}^\infty g(x)\times P_X(x)=\alpha E[ g(X)]$$
$$E[\alpha g(X) + \beta h(X)] = \alpha E[g(X)] + \beta E[h(X)]$$

$$E[\alpha] = \alpha$$

X的$n^{th}$moment:
$$E[X^n] = \sum\limits_{x=-\infty}^\infty X^n\times P_X(x)$$

#### X的变异数(variance)：
$$\sigma_X^2= E[(X-\mu)^2] = \sum\limits_{x=-\infty}^\infty （x-\mu_x)^2\times P_X(x)$$
$$\sigma_X^2 = E[X^2]-\mu_X^2$$ 

$\sigma_X^2$  **大**  则点与点之间较为分散
$\sigma_X^2$ **小**则点与点之间较为集中
![[Pasted image 20260410013410.png]]
#### 标准差(standard deviation)
$$\sigma_X = \sqrt{Variance}$$




#### X ~ Bernouli(p):
- $\mu_X =1\times p + 0\times (1-p) =p$
- $\sigma_X^2 =E[X^2] -\mu_X^2 =\sum\limits_{x=0}^1 x^2\times P_X(x) - \mu_X^2 = 1^2p +)^2(1-p) -p^2 =p(1-p)$

#### X ~ BIN(n, p):
$\mu_X = np$
$\sigma_X^2 = np(1-p)$


#### X~GEO(p):
- $\mu_X=\sum\limits_{x=0}^{\infty} x\times P_X(x) =\sum\limits_{x=0}^{\infty}x(1-p)^{x-1}p=\frac{1}{p}$
- $\sigma_X^2=E[X^2]-\mu_X^2=\frac{1-p}{p^2}$

#### X~PASKAL(k,p):
- $\mu_X=\frac{k}{p}$
- $\sigma_X^2=\frac{k(1-p)}{p^2}$


#### X~POI($\alpha$):
- $\mu_X =\alpha$
- $\sigma_X^2 =\alpha$


#### X~UNIF(a,b):
- $\mu_X =\frac{a+B}{2}$
- $\sigma_X^2 =\frac{1}{12}(b-a)(b-a+2)$

## 7-1 期望值（连续）

- 先变成离散的随机变数
对连续的随机变数X而言
X的值以$\Delta$为单位无条件舍去来近似结果：**离散随机变数Y**
- 当$\Delta \rightarrow 0, X 趋近 Y$
$n\Delta,(n+1)\Delta \rightarrow Y =n\Delta$
$E[X] =\lim\limits_{\Delta \to 0}E[Y] = \lim\limits_{\Delta \to 0}\sum\limits_{n =-\infty}^{\infty}n\Delta\times P_Y(n\Delta)$
$= \lim\limits_{\Delta \to 0}\sum\limits_{n =-\infty}^{\infty}n\Delta\times f_X(n\Delta)\Delta= \int_{-\infty}^{\infty}xf_X(x)dx$

#### 期望值 ：
$E[g(X)] =\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}g(x)f_X(x)dx$


#### X的变异数(variance)：
$$\sigma_X^2=E[(X-\mu_X)^2] =\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}(x-\mu_X)^2f_X(x)dx$$
$$\sigma_X^2 = E[X^2]-\mu_X^2$$

$\mu_X^2 =(E[X])^2$


$\sigma_X^2$小则函数集中在变异数，大则PDF比较分散(平)



#### X~Exponential($\lambda$):
$$\mu_X=\frac{1}{\lambda}$$

$$\sigma_X^2 =\frac{1}{\lambda^2}$$

#### X ~ Erlang($n,\lambda$):
$$\mu_X =\frac{n}{\lambda}$$

$$\sigma_X^2 =\frac{n}{\lambda^2}$$

#### X~ Gaussion($\mu,\sigma$):
$$\mu_X=\mu$$

$$\sigma_X^2 =\sigma^2$$

#### X~UNIF(a,b):
$$\mu_X =\frac{a+b}{2}$$

$$\sigma_X^2=\frac{1}{12}(b-a)^2$$
